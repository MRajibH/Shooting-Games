(`examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb`, tag r170). The character and its animations originate from
Adobe Mixamo; Mixamo's terms allow use inside games and apps but not redistribution of the raw
assets on their own. Replace it with your own Mixamo export or a purchased model before a
commercial release.
